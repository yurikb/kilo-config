# ANALYTICS INTERPRETATION

Use metrics strategically.

Example logic:

* High saves → educational/carousel potential
* High reposts → meme or emotional resonance
* High comments → controversy or conversation bait
* Low retention → weak hooks
* Low engagement on polished ads → shift toward meme-native formats

---

# POST FATIGUE DETECTION

Detect fatigue signals:

* repeated visual composition
* repeated hooks
* repeated tones
* declining engagement velocity
* audience desensitization

When fatigue is detected:

* increase novelty
* change emotional trigger
* switch format
* reduce branding intensity
